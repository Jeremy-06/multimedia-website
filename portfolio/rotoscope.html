Motion Design / Project Feature

Frame-by-Frame Motion Study

This project explores rotoscope-inspired motion by tracing selected frames, refining silhouettes, and synchronizing key movements with music accents. The goal is to make stylized animation feel expressive while still readable for Multimedia and Authoring subject output.

Project Reading

Core production notes

Concept

Visual direction

The visual style uses high-contrast edges and selective color to emphasize gesture and rhythm.

Workflow

Production setup

Frames were traced in batches, then composited with glow, grain, and timing adjustments in post.

Outcome

Final presentation

The final output is a short motion piece with cleaner transitions and stronger scene continuity.
