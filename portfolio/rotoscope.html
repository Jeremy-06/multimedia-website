Rotoscope Guide

Step-by-step production workflow

This rotoscope project was built in Adobe After Effects, then refined with additional editing, timing, and presentation polish in CapCut.

Steps 1-3

Build the rotoscope base

  • Import the source video into Adobe After Effects and create a new composition using the clip settings.
  • Choose the key movement sections that need tracing so the animation stays focused and readable.
  • Trace the subject frame by frame or in short frame batches, refining silhouettes, edges, and major motion shapes.
  • Add color fills, outlines, glow, or contrast effects to make the traced movement stand out from the background.
Steps 4-6

Polish and export

  • Preview the animation in After Effects and adjust timing where the traced motion feels too fast, slow, or unclear.
  • Export the After Effects composition as a video file for final editing.
  • Open the exported video in CapCut for additional editing, music synchronization, transitions, and final pacing.
  • Render the final CapCut output in a presentation-ready video format for upload and classroom viewing.
